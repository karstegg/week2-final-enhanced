ALWAYS ISOLATE & LOCKOUT BEFORE WORK

ENGINEERING
AUGUST 2025

“No job is so urgent that it cannot be isolated safely.”

CRITICAL REMINDERS

  • NEVER interfere with or remove another person's lock.
  • NEVER use common or master keys for personal lockouts.

LOCK REMOVAL AUTHORITY

Only the responsible Engineer can give permission to remove a lock if the owner is absent, and only after all safety checks are complete.

TAKE NOTE OF THE FOLLOWING:

  • Plan & Document: Before work, complete the Isolation Record Book (IRB). Ensure all equipment is correctly labelled.
  • Isolate the Power Circuit: Lock out the main power source, not the control circuit, to prevent accidental start-up.
  • Check for Secondary Energy: Be aware of ring feeds, back-feeds, or interconnected systems that could energize the equipment.
  • Dissipate Stored Energy: Safely release or restrain all residual energy (e.g., bleed, vent, block, discharge).
  • Verify Zero Energy: Always test the equipment to confirm the isolation is effective before you touch any components.
  • Your Lock, Your Life: Each person must apply their own lock. For team jobs, use hasps (calipers) to secure the isolation point.
  • Attach Your Tag: Ensure your personal tag with your name, trade, and contact details is attached to your lock.
  • Last On, First Off: The person in charge of the work is the last to remove their lock, and only after all guards are replaced and the area is safe.
