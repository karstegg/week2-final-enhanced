# Review HEAL Matrices Workflow

## Purpose
To ensure that the Highlights, Emerging Issues, Actions (Priorities), and Lowlights (HEAL) are concise, correctly categorized, and formatted.

## Steps

1.  **Check Constraints**:
    - **Highlights**: Max 6 items per site.
    - **Lowlights**: Max 4 items per site.
    - **Emerging Issues**: Max 4 items per site.
    - **Priorities**: Max 3 items per site.

2.  **Content Review**:
    - Ensure site names are abbreviated (e.g., `[N2]`, `[N3]`, `[Glo]`).
    - Ensure text is concise and executive-summary style.
    - Verify that "Actions" are forward-looking priorities.

3.  **Formatting**:
    - Ensure consistent capitalization and punctuation.
