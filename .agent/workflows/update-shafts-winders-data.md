# Update Shafts & Winders Data Workflow

## Purpose
To extract and update data for the Shafts & Winders section.

## Steps

1.  **Identify Data Source**:
    - Look for Shafts & Winders CSVs or images.

2.  **Extract Data**:
    - **Production**: Tons per hour, Rock Winder Availability.
    - **HEAL**: Highlights, Lowlights, Emerging Issues, Priorities.

3.  **Update Report Data**:
    - Update the `shaftsAndWinders` section in `src/data/reportData.ts`.
