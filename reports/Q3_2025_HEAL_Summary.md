# Q3 2025 Engineering HEAL Summary

### **Highlights (Key Wins & Progress)**
- **BEV Fleet Uptime & Stability:** Consistent week-on-week improvement in BEV performance at N3, with fleet achieving stable, above-target availability by end of quarter.
- **Shaft & Winder Milestones:** Shaft-lining repair milestones were consistently achieved ahead of plan, with multiple successful Solrock weekend work packages completed.
- **Successful Audits & Compliance:** Passed a significant DMRE Audit and made good progress with CAS Level 9 implementation, with 68% of the priority fleet fitted.
- **New Equipment Delivery & Commissioning:** Successfully delivered and commissioned two new emulsion UVs and two new scalers at Nchwaning 2, enhancing operational capability.
- **Gloria Fleet Recovery:** Key Gloria assets (loaders & scalers) were brought back into service, contributing to improved TMM availability.

### **Lowlights (Major Challenges & Incidents)**
- **Diesel Fleet Reliability:** Persistent reliability issues with the diesel DT fleet at N3 (avg. 71% availability) and significant availability dips for loaders & RTs at N2.
- **BEV Major Breakdowns:** The BEV fleet was impacted by a long-standing breakdown of DT147 (split-drive), hampering production and tying up resources.
- **Production & Plant Stoppages:** The quarter saw notable production losses, including a 25m conveyor belt tear at Gloria and multiple plant stoppages at N2.
- **Safety & Equipment Incidents:** A notable safety incident occurred with the overturn of the SR0030 Scaler at N3, triggering a high-priority investigation.

### **Emerging Issues (Risks & Strategic Concerns)**
- **Artisan & Staff Shortages:** A critical shortage of fitters and riggers in the Winder section and general labor shortages at Gloria are placing significant pressure on maintenance schedules and team capacity.
- **BEV Charging & Infrastructure Bottlenecks:** BEV turnaround is being dragged down by a limited number of chargers and intermittent faults, creating significant operational bottlenecks.
- **CAS Level 9 Post-Commissioning Faults:** Following CAS L9 commissioning, new faults are emerging on DTs (e.g., brakes not releasing) and Scalers (crawl mode issues), indicating a need for system stabilization.
- **Winter Season Impacts:** A noticeable increase in winter-related sick leave at N3 is impacting workforce availability.

### **Priorities (Focus for Next Quarter)**
- **Recruitment & Staffing:** Urgently appoint GES at N3 and Gloria and fill critical artisan vacancies in the Shafts & Winders section to stabilize team structures.
- **BEV Infrastructure & Reliability:** Improve BEV battery and charger utilization and conduct a comprehensive BEV Fire Risk Management Controls Review.
- **Resolve Critical Breakdowns & Issues:** Address the N2 MV breakers issue, complete the NCH 2 shaft lining (final concrete pour), and resolve persistent diesel fleet reliability problems.
- **CAS & Nerospec Stabilization:** Address post-commissioning issues on LDVs and other equipment to ensure the stability and reliability of the new CAS L9 system.
